# Draft consent, rating and withdrawal procedure

Not launched. Requires operator approval, jurisdiction-specific legal/ethics review
and a staffed withdrawal contact before recruitment. English and Simplified Chinese
participant materials must describe the same approved scope; a checkbox cannot
substitute for a valid release. Do not reuse Auvylen customer portraits or ratings.

## Portrait participant information (proposed wording)

“We are studying how a group of adult raters responds to photographs. Your images
may receive subjective aesthetic ratings on a 1–7 scale. This can be sensitive or
uncomfortable. These ratings are not objective assessments of you, your health or
your value. Participation is voluntary and limited to adults. You may skip optional
demographic questions, stop capture, or ask for removal using your receipt code.
Declining does not affect your account or access to other services.

“With your separate permission we will store the specified portraits with approved
cloud processors, show them privately to adult research raters, aggregate their
ratings and train/evaluate a prediction model. We will not publish your portrait,
name or individual ratings. These permissions do not include public or commercial
consumer scoring unless you separately opt in to a clearly described future use.
You are not giving permission to infer your identity or sensitive characteristics.
We explain who operates the study, processors/locations, exact retention dates,
access controls, contact details and withdrawal limits before you agree.”

Separate recorded choices: adult confirmation; photograph rights authority;
research capture and cloud processing; private human rating; research training and
derived weights; optional demographic processing. Any service use or public weight
release needs a distinct reviewed grant. No preselected boxes, bundled marketing
consent or face-based age estimation. Provide a private receipt/revocation token,
consent version, translated copy and retention deadline. Keep contact/release records
separate from pseudonymous model inputs. Compensation must not depend on appearance,
rating outcomes or giving broader optional permission.

## Independent adult raters

Explain that portraits are authorized research material and ratings are subjective.
Use the approved 1–7 anchors: 1 much less attractive than average, 4 average,
7 much more attractive than average (retain source-language equivalence). Permit
skip/exit, prohibit saving/sharing images, and do not display other ratings or model
predictions. Never ask for insulting free-text judgments. Randomize images, cap
session length, prevent own/acquaintance ratings, log consent/rater pseudonym and
predeclared quality checks. Holdout raters and labels remain with the custodian.
Store raw human responses securely; training consumes audited aggregates.

## Removal procedure and retention proposal

Before launch, replace all proposed durations and contact details with approved,
operational values. Proposed: acknowledge within 7 days; remove active source,
derivative, rating and feature records within 30 days; encrypted backup expiry
within 90 days with tombstones reapplied after restore. Retain only minimal lawful
audit evidence. Train/evaluation manifests must flag withdrawal and revoke affected
checkpoint eligibility; queue a full retrain excluding those records before reuse.
Do not promise exact machine unlearning or deletion from an already distributed
artifact. No public weights in this pilot. If downstream withdrawal cannot be
honored, explain this before consent and do not collect under incompatible terms.

Keep holdout independence when withdrawal occurs: log a new immutable exclusion
manifest, do not replace a subject based on their score or test error, and report
resulting counts/uncertainty. Suspend release if planned coverage is no longer met.
The preview UI's short upload retention is separate from these proposed study-data
terms and never grants training permission.
